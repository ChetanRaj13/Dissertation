AI data centres to add 26.3 GW to India's power demand by FY32: Govt

Subhash Narayan / Business Standard Bureau
Business Standard, New Delhi
Published: July 2026 · Reporting on Ministry of Power Parliamentary Disclosure (Rajya Sabha UQ 956)
Source: https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/ai-data-centres-add-26-3-gw-india-power-demand-fy32-govt-126072900236_1.html
Executive Summary / Key Findings
In a written reply to the Rajya Sabha (Unstarred Question No. 956 answered by Minister of State for Power Shripad Naik), the Ministry of Power revealed that artificial intelligence and data centres are projected to add 26.3 GW of additional power demand to India's grid by FY 2031-32. Based on the midterm review of the 20th Electric Power Survey (EPS) conducted by the Central Electricity Authority (CEA), the government outlined major transmission planning (envisaging ₹9.16 lakh crore under the National Electricity Plan) and emphasized that the surging data centre load will be primarily met through renewable energy additions.
