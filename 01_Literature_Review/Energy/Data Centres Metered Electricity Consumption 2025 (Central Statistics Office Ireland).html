Executive Summary / Key Findings
Official figures from Ireland's Central Statistics Office (CSO) show that metered electricity consumption by data centres reached 7,663 Gigawatt hours (GWh) in 2025, accounting for 23% of total national metered electricity. Data centre power use increased by 10% in 2025 compared with 2024, continuing an uninterrupted decade of rapid growth from just 5% of national demand in 2015 (an overall surge of more than 500%). For the second consecutive year, data centre electricity consumption exceeded the total electricity consumed by all urban dwellings in Ireland (18%), highlighting extreme grid concentration.

Key Statistical Indicators & Annual Growth Rates

% of metered electricity consumed by data centres, 5% in 2015, 22% in 2024, 23% in 2025

% of metered electricity consumed by urban dwellings, 22% in 2015, 18% in 2025

% of metered electricity consumed by rural dwellings, 12% in 2015, 9% in 2025
