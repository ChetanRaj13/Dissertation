This folder stores primary peer-reviewed literature, doctoral theses, foundational methodology templates, and reading notes supporting the dissertation.

## Archived Papers (`papers/`)

1. **`2023-GRILL-Leo-These.pdf`**: Doctoral dissertation by Leo Grill on data center energy optimization and modeling.
2. **`A_Review_on_AI-Driven_Optimization_of_Data_Center_.pdf`**: Review paper on artificial intelligence techniques for thermal, cooling, and power management in modern compute centers.

- **Nature Reviews Clean Technology (2025)**: Designing and regulating clean energy data centers.
- **Mytton (2021)**, *npj Clean Water*: Data center water consumption metrics (WUE) and environmental assessment.
