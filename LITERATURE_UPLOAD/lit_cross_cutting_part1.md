- 1. PURPOSE & RESEARCH QUESTIONS
- This workbook provides a systematic, mathematically verified, closed-sandbox evidence synthesis supporting a university dissertation on whether India's artificial intelligence (AI) and hyperscale data centre expansion is environmentally sustainable, focusing specifically on WATER consumption, withdrawal, and groundwater competition.
- Primary Research Questions:
- - RQ1: What is the direct and indirect water intensity of AI compute and modern data centre cooling systems across varying climates?
- - RQ2: To what extent are global and Indian data centres sited in baseline water-stressed and groundwater-critical basins?
- - RQ3: How do empirical water consumption realities align with corporate disclosures, state policy incentives, and local hydrological limits (e.g. CGWB 2025 assessment)?

- 3. CODES & TAGGING SYSTEM
- Strict ontological tagging is enforced across all extraction and analysis sheets to maintain epistemological rigor:
- - [S] = Stated directly in the source paper. Mandatory for every single row in the Extraction and India_Press sheets. No inference permitted.
- - [I] = Inference by the researcher. Permitted exclusively in analytical columns (e.g. 'source quality note [I]', 'Missing Categories [I]').
- - page_flag = 'PDF page only': Explicitly marks data extracted from unnumbered PDF pages (e.g. web-to-PDF printouts, unnumbered preprints, or front-matter).
- - statement_type taxonomy: measured | modelled estimate | projection | scenario | authors' opinion | stakeholder perspective | literature citation | press investigation.
